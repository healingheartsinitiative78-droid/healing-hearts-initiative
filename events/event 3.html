Volunteer Orientation Program

2027 HUYE Rwanda

The Volunteer Orientation Program welcomes individuals who are interested in supporting the work of Healing Hearts Initiative(HHI) and contributing to efforts that promote mental wellness and stronger communities.

Volunteers play an important role in helping organizations connect with communities, share information, organize activities, and create meaningful opportunities for people to access support.

Everyone has something to contribute

Whether through community outreach, communication, organization, creativity, technical skills, or simply a willingness to serve, every contribution can make a difference.

Why Volunteers Matter

Community-based work becomes stronger when people come together around a shared purpose. Volunteers help us extend our reach and build meaningful connections with communities.

Through volunteering, individuals can also develop new skills, work with others, understand community needs, and take part in initiatives that create positive social impact.

What the Orientation Covers

Understanding the mission and values of Healing Hearts Initiative
Learning about available volunteer opportunities
Understanding responsible and respectful community engagement
Learning how volunteers can support mental health awareness activities
Understanding teamwork and effective communication
Exploring ways to contribute skills and ideas

Building a Culture of Service

We believe volunteering should be a meaningful experience built on respect, teamwork, responsibility, and a genuine commitment to serving communities.

Our orientation helps new volunteers understand how they can contribute while respecting the dignity, privacy, and experiences of the people and communities we serve.

Our Goal

Our goal is to build a committed community of volunteers who are ready to contribute their time, skills, ideas, and energy toward promoting mental wellness and creating positive change.

If you are passionate about helping others and want to contribute to a healthier and more supportive community, we welcome you to learn more about volunteering with Healing Hearts Initiative.
