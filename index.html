ABOUT US

Healing Hearts.
Empowering Minds. Building Hope.

Healing Hearts Initiative(HHI) is a youth-led organization based in Rwanda dedicated to promoting mental health, psychosocial wellbeing, and youth empowerment.

The Healing Hearts Initiative(HHI)was established in May 2025 to respond to the growing need for mental health awareness, emotional support, and psychosocial services among young people and communities in Rwanda. The initiative creates safe and inclusive spaces where youth can openly express themselves, access accurate mental health information, and receive appropriate psychosocial support.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission & Vision

MISSION

To promote mental and emotional well-being among young people by creating safe spaces, breaking stigma, empowering youth, connecting them to support, and conducting research.

VISION

A community where mental health is prioritized and every young person feels safe, supported, empowered to heal, thrive, and seek help when needed.
