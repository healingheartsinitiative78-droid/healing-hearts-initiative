WHO WE ARE

Who We Are?

Healing Hearts Initiative (HHI) is a youth-focused mental health and psychosocial support initiative founded in Rwanda. We bring together young people, volunteers, mental health professionals, and community partners who believe that every young person deserves a supportive environment to grow and thrive.

Our work is youth-centered, inclusive, community-based, and informed by both research and professional guidance. We combine peer support, safe spaces, community engagement, and collaboration with professionals to make mental health support more accessible to young people and their families.

We are a growing community of people who believe in listening, supporting, learning, and healing together.

OUR STORY

Every Great Change Starts With One Heart

Healing Hearts Initiative (HHI) was founded in May 2025 by Diane NYIRARUKUNDO, with a vision to support young people's mental and emotional well-being.

May 2025

The Beginning

Healing Hearts Initiative (HHI) was founded by Diane NYIRARUKUNDO, driven by a vision to support young people's mental and emotional well-being and create spaces where they can feel safe, supported, and empowered.

October 2025

Welcoming Our Co-Founder

In October 2025, Prosper MAZIMPAKA joined HHI as Co-Founder. This marked an important step in strengthening our leadership and advancing our commitment to youth mental health and emotional well-being.

November 2025

Building Our Executive Committee

As HHI continued to grow, we welcomed more people into our Executive Committee, bringing together different skills, experiences, and strengths to guide our activities and advance our mission.

2026

Establishing Our Advisory Board

In 2026, we established an Advisory Board of mental health professionals who provide mentorship, guidance, and expertise to help strengthen our work and support our mission.

Today

Growing Together, Creating Impact

Today, HHI has grown to include digital and physical safe spaces, dedicated volunteers, and professionals we collaborate with to support our mission.

So far, we have reached more than 50,000 young people, 50 communities, and 50 Rwandan families. We believe supporting young people also means supporting the families around them, especially their parents, who play an important role in their well-being.

We are grateful for the growing impact we are making in the community and remain committed to creating spaces where every young person feels safe, supported, and empowered to heal, thrive, and seek help when needed.

WHY WE EXIST

Every Young Person Deserves Hope, Support & Opportunity

Many young people struggle with stigma, limited access to reliable mental health information, unhealthy coping, and delays in getting the support they need. Many also lack safe spaces where they can openly express themselves without judgment.

Healing Hearts Initiative exists to change this. We create safe and inclusive spaces where young people can express themselves, learn healthy coping strategies, access accurate mental health information, and feel safe seeking support. We break the stigma around mental health and connect young people to appropriate help before their struggles become crises.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Find answers to common questions about mental health, emotional well-being, and seeking professional support.

Mental health is about how we think, feel, behave, relate to others, and cope with life. Just like physical health, mental health can be good or poor, and everyone has mental health—not only people with mental disorders.

A mental health problem can be a period of emotional or psychological difficulty, such as prolonged stress, grief, or anxiety. A mental disorder is a clinically recognized condition involving significant changes in thoughts, emotions, behavior, or functioning that may require professional assessment and treatment. Not every mental health problem is a mental disorder.
