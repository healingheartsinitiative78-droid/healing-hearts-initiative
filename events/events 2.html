Community Mental Health Workshop

2027 NDERA Rwanda

The Community Mental Health Workshop is designed to create an open and supportive environment where community members can learn more about mental health and discuss the challenges that can affect emotional well-being.

Healing Hearts Initiative(HHI) believes that mental health awareness should reach people where they live, learn, work, and connect. By encouraging honest conversations, communities can become better equipped to recognize concerns early and support people with compassion.

Mental health belongs in every community

Creating spaces for understanding and conversation can help reduce stigma and encourage people to seek appropriate support when they need it.

Why This Workshop Matters

Mental health challenges can affect people of different ages and backgrounds. However, stigma, misinformation, and limited awareness can make it difficult for individuals and families to recognize when someone may need support.

This workshop encourages participants to look beyond misconceptions and develop a more compassionate understanding of mental health.

What Participants Will Explore

Understanding basic concepts of mental health
Recognizing signs that someone may need support
Understanding the effects of stigma and misinformation
Learning how to respond with empathy and respect
Understanding when and where to seek professional support
Strengthening community support and inclusion

Building Open Conversations

We want communities to become places where people feel comfortable talking about their emotional well-being without being judged. Open conversations can help families, friends, young people, and community leaders better understand mental health and encourage appropriate support.

Our Goal

Our goal is to promote understanding, reduce stigma, encourage early support, and strengthen communities where people feel seen, heard, and supported.

Healing Hearts Initiative(HHI) continues to work with communities to promote mental wellness, create safe spaces, and connect people with appropriate support.
