Youth Leadership Training

2027 NYAKARIRO Rwanda

The Youth Leadership Training is an opportunity for young people to strengthen their leadership skills while developing a deeper understanding of mental wellness, emotional resilience, and positive community engagement.

At Healing Hearts Initiative(HHI), we believe that young people have an important role to play in creating healthier and more supportive communities. Leadership is not only about taking responsibility; it is also about listening, understanding others, communicating with empathy, and knowing how to support people around us.

Leadership with empathy

Strong leadership begins with understanding, respect, compassion, and the ability to create a supportive environment for others.

Why This Training Matters

Young people often face academic, social, family, and personal challenges while navigating an important stage of life. Developing leadership and emotional skills can help young people respond to challenges in healthier ways and become positive influences within their communities.

The training also encourages young people to recognize the importance of mental wellness and to understand when it is appropriate to seek help or encourage someone else to seek support.

What Participants Will Explore

Understanding positive leadership and responsibility
Building effective communication and teamwork skills
Developing emotional awareness and healthy coping skills
Understanding the importance of peer support
Promoting safe and inclusive spaces for young people
Identifying ways to create positive change in the community
Our Goal

Our goal is to support young people to become confident, compassionate, and responsible leaders who can contribute to healthier communities while also taking care of their own mental well-being.

Healing Hearts Initiative(HHI) continues to create opportunities where young people can learn, connect, share experiences, and develop the skills needed to make a positive difference.
