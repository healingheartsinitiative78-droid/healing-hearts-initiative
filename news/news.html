Healing Hearts Initiative Launches Mental Health Awareness Campaign Across Schools

Hundreds of students participated in interactive workshops focused on mental wellness, emotional resilience, and peer support. The campaign aims to reduce stigma around mental health while empowering young people to seek help and support one another.

Author Healing Hearts Initiative
Reading Time 5 min read
Published July 12, 2026

Mental health challenges among young people continue to increase, yet many students still struggle in silence due to stigma and limited access to mental health education. Healing Hearts Initiative launched this campaign to create safe spaces where students could openly discuss emotional wellbeing, build resilience, and learn where to seek help.

The Challenge

Across many schools, conversations about mental health remain limited. Students often experience stress, anxiety, depression, and social pressure without knowing how to manage these challenges or where to find support.

Through consultations with educators, parents, and community leaders, Healing Hearts Initiative identified an urgent need for awareness programs that empower young people with knowledge and practical coping skills.

What We Did

Our team organized interactive workshops in secondary schools across Kigali, bringing together students, teachers, counselors, and volunteers.

Participants learned about emotional wellbeing, recognizing warning signs, supporting friends, and accessing professional mental health services.

  • Interactive mental health sessions
  • Peer support discussions
  • Stress management activities
  • Counseling awareness
  • Question-and-answer sessions

Mental health is just as important as physical health. Every young person deserves to feel heard, supported, and empowered to thrive.

Healing Hearts Initiative

Community Impact

The campaign reached hundreds of students and created meaningful conversations about emotional wellbeing. Teachers reported increased student engagement, while participants expressed greater confidence in seeking support when facing mental health challenges.

Looking Ahead

Healing Hearts Initiative plans to expand the campaign to additional schools and communities, strengthen partnerships with education stakeholders, and continue promoting mental health awareness throughout Rwanda.
