  • Psychosocial support and resilience activities
  • Youth-friendly wellbeing education
  • Referral and community support pathways

  • Youth-led community initiatives

07

Research & Evidence

Our Research & Evidence program generates practical knowledge that helps communities, partners, and programs better understand needs, measure results, learn what works, and improve future interventions.

  • Community needs assessments and baseline studies
  • Surveys, interviews, focus groups, and data collection
  • Program monitoring, evaluation, and learning
  • Data analysis, reports, briefs, and knowledge products
  • Partnerships with universities and research institutions
  • Youth participation in research and evidence generation
Research findings will be used to strengthen program design, identify community priorities, and support evidence-informed decisions.
RESEARCH & EVIDENCE

Turning Community Questions Into Useful Evidence

Our research focus is designed to help HHI understand community priorities, improve programs, measure results, and share lessons with partners and communities.

Community Needs

Identify priorities, barriers, experiences, and service gaps through community-informed research.

Monitoring & Evaluation

Track activities, outputs, outcomes, and lessons to understand whether programs are meeting their objectives.

Youth Participation

Where appropriate and safe, involve young people in identifying questions, collecting perspectives, and interpreting findings.

Knowledge Products

Turn findings into reports, briefs, presentations, learning materials, and practical recommendations.

RESEARCH METHODS

How We Generate Evidence

Depending on the research question, HHI may use qualitative, quantitative, or mixed approaches. Methods can include surveys, interviews, focus groups, community consultations, document review, routine program data, and program evaluation.

  • Baseline and needs assessments
  • Surveys and structured data collection
  • Interviews and focus group discussions
  • Monitoring, evaluation, and learning
  • Data analysis and evidence synthesis
  • Research reports and policy/practice briefs

Ethics, Privacy & Participant Protection

Research involving people must protect dignity, privacy, safety, and informed participation. Projects will follow applicable ethical and data-protection requirements and seek appropriate review or approval when required. For adolescent research, participation and protection will be handled according to applicable consent/assent and safeguarding requirements.
